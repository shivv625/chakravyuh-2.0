The Legend & The Challenge

Seven Rings. Thirty-Six Hours. One Breakthrough.

In the Mahabharata, the Chakravyuh was a formidable seven-ring spiral formation designed to test strategy, courage, and clarity under pressure. Chakravyuh 2.0 brings that spirit to modern engineering at GITA Autonomous College, Bhubaneswar—challenging India's sharpest student builders to pierce seven progressive gates from ideation to live deployment.

Hosted in association with Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT), Odisha, the hackathon unites 500+ innovators for 36 hours of mentorship, rapid prototyping, and high-stakes evaluation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question to expand
Q1 Is there a registration fee for shortlisted teams? brightness_7

Idea submission is completely free. Shortlisted teams pay a nominal ₹400 per member hospitality fee covering all meals, snacks, and campus accommodation for 36 hours.

Q2 Can we use pre-trained AI models or public APIs? brightness_7

Yes, open-weight models and public APIs are permitted, provided your core application logic, integration, and product workflow are built during the hackathon.

Q3 Will travel reimbursement or certificates be provided? brightness_7

Every finalist receives an official BPUT-recognized participation certificate, swag kit, and mentor feedback. Travel support is available for outstation winners.
